I hack Super Famicom games into French and write my own tools for it, [a816](https://github.com/manz/a816) to assemble, [kintsuki](https://github.com/manz/kintsuki) to debug. [FF4](https://lab.ringum.net/projects/ff4/) is the one I started at Terminus Traduction at the end of 2000, it went unfinished for over twenty years and is now translated all the way through, with a first beta out in October 2026. Bahamut Lagoon and DQ6 are in progress, and Chikuma is on the side, the iPod classic's firmware rebuilt from Apple's RetailOS.
